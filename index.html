Why Information Technology?

     I took Information Technology because of my growing interest in computers. This course was actually my 2nd option next to Mechanical Engineering until Grade 12 when I finally chose Information Technology as my 1st option. I saw myself working with and using computers. I first became interested in this during junior high school. We learned about HTML and CSS during grade 9 in our ICT class under ma'am Banglos. And we learned about the C++ language during grade 10 in our ICT class still under ma’am Banglos (thank you ma'am). While others saw these classes as hard and complicated, I was having so much fun learning and interacting with the computer through these computer languages. After grade 10, I became interested in Mechanical Engineering after a guidance career job results thingy actually showed I was not interested too much in Computers. So during Senior High School, I was set on a new course and worked towards achieving it. But after the 2nd semester of Grade 11 taking Basic Calculus, I instantly knew that I did not want to become an Engineer of any kind. While I didn’t fail the class, I was disqualified from honors because of this class which is a failure of its own kind. I did redeem myself in grade 12 and I did love our physics class, it did not outweigh my hatred for basic calculus. Because of that one class, now I’m here and I’m so grateful that I chose this course instead of engineering.
