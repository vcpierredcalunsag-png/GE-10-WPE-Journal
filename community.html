COMMUNITY

ROTC Blood Donation

This was my first time donating blood. The opportunity came up and after making sure that I was not anemic, generally healthy, able to donate, and consent from parents, I grabbed the opportunity to help those in need and simultaneously get 4 pts for GE 10. About the actual experience, I was genuinely scared. We were pulled out from the formation from the start so that those that will donate blood could get rest and hydrated. We were stationed just next to the RTY. The atmosphere seemed light, but it also seemed very dawning just because they really dragged out the process and it took a very long time for us to actually donate. Most of the time was just waiting for our turn. The collecting of the blood only took around 10-15 mins and another 10-15 mins rest. And when I saw the needle that they would use on me, I legitimately almost backed out. It was really big and I didn’t think it would fit. But nonetheless, I mustered up the courage to continue and push through with it and I am thankful that I did. I hope that my blood donation helped someone in need. I hope that they never need to use it, but it is there incase.


Community Feeding

The community feeding event was the 2nd community event that I was a part of. I was a sub organizer, helping to handle and oversee the food committee. My role was utilized on the day and it was more so on the actual event rather than the planning which was mostly the logistics and food committee. This event was really nice for me because I got to see a bunch of kids and we weren’t just there to feed them, but to also play games and have fun with the kids. I remember having so much fun during the games portion, especially because the hosts of the event really did a good job at playing with the kids and everyone was flexible and adjusted to the roles needed at the time. This also made me realize what a privilege it is to be here at Silliman University. I just had a newfound appreciation for my parents and for the school that I have been going to for my whole life.


ROTC Rice Donation

The SUROTC unit called for donations for those affected by the recent earthquakes and floods in Cebu. Alongside the rice, other battalions donated money as well. I donated 5kg of rice and I hope that it makes a difference in the lives of those affected by the recent natural disasters.


GE 10 Donation

For our GE 10, we had an activity to donate to orphans. This was announced the night before and the one thing that amazed me was how quickly, not just our group but all of the groups, were able to gather the materials and supplies needed to donate and decorate our “gift baskets/boxes” just overnight. This activity proves that we can and are able to provide extra supplies to the less fortunate. I hope that there will still be continuous outlets for, not just CCS, but for all colleges to have an outlet to donate their extra materials and supplies just like this event and the event organized by the SUSG.
