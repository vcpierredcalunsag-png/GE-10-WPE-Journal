Badminton Tournament

The Badminton tournament was very stressful to organize, and I even lost money organizing the tournament. I spent almost 1k on reserving the court, buying the shuttlecock for the players to use, and I also included myself in the registration so that there could be more money for the tournament prize. There was a registration fee of 75php for the players, and there were 10 players. The tournament was bracket style, 1 game, doubles, and the teams were randomized. The players were late (were Filipino, of course we have a different time zone), but thankfully the tournament finished on time. Riley Villegas and June Zaragoza ended up winning the tournament and split their cash prize of 500php. I ended up losing around 600 pesos and I truly hope that this event counts toward the court.


Billiards Tournament

The Billiards tournament was organized by Jay Matthew, a fellow CCS freshman. We were playing at Daniels, a billiards place near Cafe Racer and Marina. I could have been part of a committee for the tournament, but I didn’t have the time to be there early and finish the tournament. I was there for about 2 hours and played 2 games with my teammate, Thursday Cabarubbias. I couldn’t finish the whole night because I had a commitment at 6pm so Felvince played on my behalf. The tournament was fun. Im not a very good billiards player, but it was the atmosphere and the people we were playing with was what made the tournament very fun and memorable. Edsel was there, and he didn’t know how to play or hold the cue stick. It was nice to hang out with the fellow CCS freshies outside of school.


Chess Tournament

The Chess Tournament was held at the ground floor of the UCS building just outside the office. There were around 10 players and the event lasted from 10:30am until the mental health event at the Luce auditorium in the afternoon. I played a total of 5 games and won 4 games and lost only one game, and that was against Edsel Anthony. He is a very strong player and maybe he could represent CCS for intrams in the future. My favorite game was in fact against Edsel because he talks a lot. Like so much. It was fun to play against him because he really took the game very seriously and really analyzed everything. For sure, I wanted to win, but I had more fun playing against Edsel than playing the game.
